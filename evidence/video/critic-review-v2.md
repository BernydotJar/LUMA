# Video Showcase Critic Review v2

## Verdict

PASS_WITH_PRIVATE_RIGHTS_GATE

The client-visible superuser surface demonstrates video through a public Seres de Excelencia source.

The private course corpus is not exposed, embedded or transformed in this release.

The implementation preserves the learning boundary:
- watching is exposure evidence;
- capability requires subsequent practice;
- private derivative generation requires authorization for content and identifiable people;
- no private Drive locator appears in the client surface.

This is suitable for the showcase while the private derivative pipeline remains gated.
