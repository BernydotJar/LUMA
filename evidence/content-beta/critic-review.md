# Beta Content Expansion — Critic Review

## Verdict

PASS_FOR_FIRST_USER_BETA

## What improved

- LUMA now contains seven learner experiences rather than a single demo practice.
- The primary learner information architecture remains capability-first rather than module-first.
- Module 2 content is limited to practical communication concepts supported by the supplied source.
- Claims with weak or high-risk causal framing were deliberately excluded from learner-facing content.
- Module 3 expands into P.A.S., logical levels, beliefs, values and identity.
- Every experience ends in application or reflection rather than passive completion.
- Reflective completion emits a self-reported practice event and explicitly avoids claiming mastery.
- The existing P.A.S. scored simulation remains available for stronger application evidence.

## Product risks still open

- Most new experiences currently collect reflective evidence rather than scored transfer evidence.
- The next content increment should add at least one structured simulation for congruence or calibration.
- First-user qualitative feedback should drive which experience receives deeper interaction first.

## Decision

Suitable for the first external tester and client demo.
