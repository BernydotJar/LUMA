# Themed Superuser Experience v2 — Critic Review

## Scope

Adversarial product review of:
- Seres de Excelencia, Liquid Light and Nocturne themes;
- superuser role comparison;
- source-guided iconography and generated 3D still candidate;
- liquid-glass material rules;
- andragogical progress;
- original coach-voice prototype;
- public video showcase plus private-video rights boundary.

## Findings

### PASS — Three themes are product expressions, not three products

The theme switch changes palette, material, icon treatment and emphasis while preserving:
- learning state;
- role permissions;
- evidence semantics;
- source truth.

### PASS — Seres de Excelencia reads institutionally

The official SE logo and measured blue / transformation pink / plum palette are visible without using brand color as a gender classifier.

### PASS — Liquid Glass is governed

The material contract prohibits glass-on-glass and requires:
- restrained transparency;
- readable text;
- concentric geometry;
- reduced-motion fallback;
- reduced-transparency fallback.

The automated adversarial matrix reports zero blocking findings across 30 theme / route / viewport checks.

### PASS — Progress remains adult

Tu progreso expresses:
- capability demonstrated;
- transfer evidence;
- next demonstration.

It does not lead with points, streaks, trophies or child-oriented rewards.

### PASS WITH GATE — Gemini 3D still

One SE Practice/P.A.S. still was generated through the authenticated Gemini bridge.

It reads as adult conceptual art and preserves the refractive-prism metaphor.

However:
- the generated JPEG did not contain native alpha;
- transparency was derived locally;
- the still remains a review candidate;
- animation is blocked until Product Owner approval of the still and then a separate motion approval.

The deterministic SVG remains the production baseline.

### PASS WITH GATE — Voice

The 12.4 second Seres Coach Voice prototype proves:
- speech delivery;
- in-product playback;
- Spanish pacing contract.

It does not establish the final Colombian / Latin American voice identity.

Direct imitation or cloning of Mary Cardona Lenis remains blocked without explicit rights.

### PASS WITH GATE — Video

The superuser surface can play a public Seres de Excelencia video without exposing private Drive locations.

The private course corpus remains behind the rights gate. Video completion is exposure evidence only.

## Residual product risks

1. The SE and Liquid Light themes intentionally share a bright premium foundation; further institutional differentiation should be driven by client review, not arbitrary decoration.
2. The generated icon still needs explicit PO approval before it can replace the source-controlled SVG.
3. The final voice requires accent/tone review and commercial rights confirmation.
4. Private class derivatives require identifiable-person/demo consent before materialization.

## Verdict

PASS_FOR_CLIENT_SHOWCASE.
