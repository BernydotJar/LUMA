# SPEC 010 — Critic Review

## Review question

Does LUMA now separate the coachee experience from internal coach intelligence while preserving the adaptive-learning value?

## Findings repaired

### Negative framing in the learner experience

The learner home previously defined value by contrast:
- "Hoy no necesitas otra lección."
- "No sigues módulos."
- "Sin contenido de relleno."
- "La teoría no es el problema."

The revised experience states the intended action directly:
- "Hoy llevas lo que sabes a la práctica."
- "Tu journey avanza con lo que demuestras."
- "Una práctica enfocada en transferencia."
- "Base conceptual lista."

The recommendation engine explanations were also rewritten so the learner-facing reason is constructive and action-oriented.

### Internal-model leakage

The learner navigation previously exposed:
- Twin;
- technical Content Intelligence.

The revised learner navigation contains:
- Hoy;
- Journey;
- Práctica;
- LUMA.

Browser verification confirms the /learn surface contains no visible "Learning Twin" copy.

### Learning Twin audience

The Learning Twin is now coach-facing at:

/studio/learners/mariana

The legacy /twin route redirects to the coach view.

The coach view exposes:
- dimensions;
- evidence;
- observed vs inferred vs self-reported provenance;
- confidence;
- coach note;
- scenario projection;
- model governance.

### Proprietary marking

The coach view visibly displays:

© 2026 LUMA · Learning Twin model & evidence framework · Proprietary coach view.

This is a product/IP notice. It should not be interpreted as a legal conclusion about the protectability of an abstract idea.

### Visual mode separation

Coachee:
- warm cream;
- burgundy;
- burnt orange;
- muted avocado;
- action-first density.

Coach:
- deep burgundy / aubergine;
- mustard intelligence accent;
- denser analytical surfaces;
- explicit internal/proprietary context.

The modes share typography, geometry, glass treatment, and motion language, so they remain one product family.

### Content Intelligence and Coach Studio

The technical Library is now rendered in coach mode as Content Intelligence.

Coach Studio and Content Intelligence were re-skinned to the same coach design system.

Negative/contrast-oriented headings were rewritten where they were product copy rather than safety or exercise content.

## Deliberately retained negative language

Negative language remains when it is the semantic content being taught or a safety claim must be precise.

Examples:
- P.A.S. exercise distractors;
- high-stakes medical claim blocking;
- source-derived statements.

These are not marketing/product-positioning copy.

## Verdict

PASS.

The role separation is materially clearer, the coachee surface is action-oriented, and the Learning Twin is now presented as proprietary coach intelligence rather than learner-facing machinery.
