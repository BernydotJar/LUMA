# Andragogical Progress Review

## Product question

Does the new progress surface help an adult learner understand useful progress without reverting to school-like points, streaks or badges?

## Information hierarchy

The surface answers:

1. What can I do now?
2. Where did I apply it?
3. What will I demonstrate next?

## Adult-learning alignment

- capability is stated in plain language;
- evidence refers to a realistic situation;
- transfer is more important than completion;
- the next action has immediate relevance;
- learner control is preserved through selectable cards;
- progress avoids personality labels and raw internal Twin scores.

## Visual review

The card/depth interaction borrows spatial organization from premium micro-interaction libraries, but retains standard tabs, focus states, readable text and reduced-motion behavior.

## Verdict

PASS for the client showcase.
