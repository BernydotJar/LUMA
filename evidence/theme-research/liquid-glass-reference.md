# Liquid Glass Reference Audit

## User-supplied reference

The supplied reference brief describes a frame-accurate Apple-style Liquid Glass motion system and establishes the following principles:

- glass behaves as a lens rather than frosted plastic;
- refraction is strongest near the rim;
- a restrained top-left specular rim and soft contact shadow establish depth;
- glass does not sit on glass;
- inner and outer corner radii are concentric;
- active color is reserved for on/selected states;
- direct manipulation, pointer magnetism and controlled springs communicate physicality;
- reduced-motion behavior must remain valid;
- icon weight and optical size remain consistent;
- the result should avoid template/Dribbble-clone aesthetics.

## LUMA adoption

LUMA adopts:

- material hierarchy;
- restrained transparency;
- active-state discipline;
- reduced-motion and reduced-transparency fallbacks;
- concentric-radius review;
- glass-on-glass adversarial testing;
- physical, interruptible interaction as a future motion standard.

LUMA does not reproduce:

- Apple product UI;
- Dynamic Island;
- SF Symbols;
- exact Apple screens;
- the supplied 32-beat animation sequence.

## Product decision

The reference is used as a material and motion quality bar for Liquid Light, not as a template to clone.
