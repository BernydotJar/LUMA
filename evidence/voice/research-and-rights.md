# Voice Research and Rights Evidence

## Public reference research

The official Seres de Excelencia ecosystem identifies the founder as **Mary Cardona Lenis**. Public program language consistently emphasizes transformation, awareness, direct instruction and a close coaching relationship.

These broad qualities informed the original voice brief:

- mature;
- Colombian / Latin American;
- warm;
- direct;
- close;
- reflective;
- authoritative without being punitive.

## Identity safety boundary

The brief intentionally excludes:

- voiceprint imitation;
- distinctive signature cadence;
- direct cloning;
- any claim that the generated voice is Mary Cardona Lenis.

A direct clone is blocked without explicit consent and rights evidence.

## Aside investigation

Aside was reviewed as a local-first transcription/dictation product. Useful ideas:

- capture can remain local;
- transcript-first workflow;
- notes can stay portable;
- no meeting bot is required.

Aside is not being treated as the speech-synthesis or voice-cloning provider for LUMA.

## Provider status

No ElevenLabs connector was available through the current ChatGPT plugin directory, and no voice provider is committed in the product architecture.

Runway is connected and can support spoken voiceover prototypes, but the first prototype remains provider-neutral until quality, latency, consent, retention and cost are tested.

## Decision

PASS for an original voice research direction.

BLOCKED for a direct identifiable voice clone until documented rights and consent are available.
