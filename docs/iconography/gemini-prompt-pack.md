## Semantic prompts

### Practice — prism

> A triangular refractive prism that receives one narrow ray and separates it into two clear interpretation paths, then resolves into a single focused path. The object represents detecting and reframing an automatic thought.

### Progress — orbital field

> A central polished sphere with three restrained orbital rings and three small satellites at distinct distances. The object represents demonstrated capability, transfer and confidence moving as related signals.

### Coach Insight — strata

> Four elegant architectural planes stacked at different heights, with one selected layer subtly illuminated. The object represents locating the most useful level of intervention.

### Human Intervention — bridge

> Two grounded sculptural forms connected by one exact luminous bridge. One side represents system evidence, the other human context. The span is stable, minimal and intentional.

### Voice — resonance

> A transparent resonance chamber containing a measured waveform made of eight sculptural bars. It represents speaking and listening as a learning modality, not a microphone app.

### Video — editorial frame

> A premium cinematic frame with one precise play aperture, a chapter timeline and a small evidence marker. It represents a source segment becoming an active learning object.
