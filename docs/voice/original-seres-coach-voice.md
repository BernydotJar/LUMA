# Original Seres Coach Voice

## Working direction

The client reference is Mary Cardona Lenis. The product goal is an **original Seres Coach Voice** informed by broad performance qualities—not a recognizable copy of an individual’s voice.

## Performance brief

- language: Latin American Spanish;
- regional fit: Colombian, broadly understandable;
- age impression: mature adult;
- presence: warm, close and composed;
- authority: direct, never scolding;
- pace: conversational, with reflective pauses;
- diction: clear without announcer stiffness;
- emotional range: grounded and natural;
- coaching behavior: asks concise questions, leaves space and avoids over-explaining;
- learning behavior: emphasizes the learner’s own words and actions.

## Anti-brief

Avoid:

- direct imitation of Mary Cardona Lenis;
- copied signature cadence or vocal mannerisms;
- celebrity-style voice cloning;
- breathy wellness clichés;
- childish enthusiasm;
- synthetic announcer delivery;
- emotional manipulation;
- diagnosis from vocal tone.

## Sample line direction

### Warm opening

> Tómate unos segundos. Cuéntame qué ocurrió, usando solamente lo que podríamos observar.

### Direct coaching question

> ¿Qué pensamiento apareció justo antes de esa emoción?

### Reinforcement

> Ahí está la diferencia: describiste el hecho y luego identificaste la interpretación.

### Next application

> Ahora probemos la misma capacidad en un contexto distinto.

## Rights and consent

An original commissioned/synthetic voice requires commercial terms, retention review and clear provider rights.

A direct clone of any identifiable person requires explicit written authorization, purpose limitation, revocation terms, provider verification and legal review. The clone path remains blocked until all evidence is supplied.

## Product use

Use the voice only when speaking or listening is pedagogically useful:

- communication scenarios;
- oral retrieval;
- revised spoken response;
- spoken reflection for a coach context pack.

Text-only completion remains available.
