# LUMA Twin Intelligence

> **Personalized learning. Human-led.**

## Product proposition

**LUMA Twin Intelligence** is an evidence-based learning intelligence layer for academies, enterprises and universities. It links two controlled digital representations:

- **Learner Twin:** a rebuildable, explainable model of demonstrated learning, goals, confidence and uncertainty. The learner can correct information; completion alone never constitutes mastery.
- **Teaching Twin:** a versioned, educator-approved teaching method, rubric and source pack. It does not imitate a person's personality, likeness or voice.

The Pedagogical Intelligence Engine connects both within institution/program authorization. The educator remains responsible for instruction, intervention, evaluation and certification.

## Differentiation

Not a course-generation chatbot. The product explicitly couples:

1. verified longitudinal learner evidence,
2. an approved instructor's teachable methodology,
3. source-grounded interventions,
4. transparent uncertainty and learner corrections,
5. educators in the decision loop,
6. controlled cost and institutional privacy boundaries.

## Success metrics

**North Star:** verified gain in demonstrated capability per effective learning time.

**Primary exploratory metric:** paired criterion-based gain differential relative to normal support.

**Adoption:** percent of assigned participants with a completed baseline/post pair, educator acceptance/override rate, percentage of proposed interventions with a documented human outcome.

**Safety:** unauthorized cross-scope access = 0; unsupported mastery update = 0; sensitive raw information transmitted to unapproved inference providers = 0.

**Economics:** invoiced model usage + hosting + fully loaded educator time, divided by active learners, plus tail latency and case escalation rate.

## Current P0 product boundary

The P0 source implementation delivers a deterministic tutor decision *proposal*, teacher-approved method revisions and an auditable human intervention workflow. It does not yet deliver a production-facing Studio UI, generative classroom assistant, actual two-institution UAT, provider-cost reconciliation or a measured educational benefit.

No model-provider endpoints are activated. Gemma 4 / DeepSeek BYOK / Vertex are swappable future infrastructure choices, not intellectual property of LUMA.

## Human-first invariant

**The objective is not fewer teachers. It is better-informed, more effective human teaching for each learner.**

No autonomous student labeling, mental-health diagnosis, unilateral certification changes, commercial manipulation or unauthorized tutor impersonation.
