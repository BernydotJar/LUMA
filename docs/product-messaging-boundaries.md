# Product Messaging Boundaries

## Rule

LUMA should communicate outcomes at the surface and mechanisms at the appropriate depth.

## Coachee surface

Show:
- what to do;
- why it fits;
- what is progressing;
- where help is available.

Do not lead with:
- Learning Twin;
- Graph;
- RAG;
- retrieval;
- reflection pipeline;
- model confidence;
- prompt versions.

## Coach surface

Show mechanisms only when they improve a decision:
- evidence;
- confidence;
- observed vs inferred;
- Learning Twin;
- provenance;
- intervention options.

## Buyer / client-demo surface

Primary value:

> De contenido experto a inteligencia de aprendizaje.

Supporting transformation:

> LUMA conecta conocimiento, práctica y evidencia para construir journeys relevantes para cada persona.

Trust layer:

> Revisión experta · Procedencia preservada · Cambios trazables.

## Internal / architecture

Allowed:
- AI proposal workflow;
- human approval gates;
- graph topology;
- prompt/pipeline versions;
- reflection execution;
- retrieval intent;
- evaluation harness.

These are product moat and governance evidence, not the primary product headline.

## Specific anti-pattern

### Avoid

> La IA propone. El experto publica.

Why:
- frames LUMA as a generic LLM workflow;
- suggests untrusted output as the default;
- foregrounds human QA cost;
- exposes the operating model;
- makes the product mentally substitutable with ChatGPT/Claude/Copilot.

### Replace with

> De contenido experto a inteligencia de aprendizaje.

and

> Cada concepto conserva su fuente, contexto y criterio de calidad para convertirse en experiencias de aprendizaje precisas.

Governance detail can remain lower in the hierarchy:

> Revisión experta · Procedencia preservada · Cambios trazables.
