# LUMA — Executive Proposal

**Status:** VERIFIED as product architecture in source. External merchant activation and business results NOT YET VERIFIED.

## Problem and opportunity

Premium education runs live, asynchronous and hybrid. Enrollment and content consumption do not demonstrate that an individual can apply what was taught. Coaches require reliable evidence and timely follow-up, and operators need visibility into activation, attendance, practice and actual learner outcomes.

LUMA is an **AI-supported Learning Intelligence layer**, connecting verified access to guided practice, human coaching and an evidence-backed Learning Twin. **Hotmart + LUMA + Stripe where economically appropriate.** We do not require migration away from existing Hotmart checkout.

## Value by decision-maker

- CEO: inspect verified activation, progress and intervention effectiveness without inventing causal revenue uplift.
- Commercial: preserve negotiated merchant economics and existing acquisition flow.
- Coach: see "Who needs me today?" with understandable signs of inactivity and evaluated practice friction.
- Learner: buy, access the program, attend live sessions, practice, reflect and receive guided help.
- Technology/privacy: separate provider-specific webhook details from learning, with Firestore ledger, verified identities, coach scope and event tracing.
- Finance: enter **actual effective** merchant fees and compare scenarios with shared assumptions and explicit omissions.

## Packages (commercial concepts, not contractual prices)

**LUMA Intelligence:** external checkout + platform fee + included monthly active learners + marginal activity + AI fair use. Included active learners do NOT imply a technical maximum.

**LUMA Commerce + Intelligence:** optional direct Stripe channel + Stripe processing + platform fee + potentially separately negotiated transaction component; never force migration.

## Pilot proposal

ASSUMED design: 1 live/hybrid program; 50–100 eligible learners; 60 days; Hotmart as checkout; LUMA onboarding, Learning Twin, AI tutoring and coach intervention. Agree on baselines, support, privacy, effective pricing, outcomes, data rights and remediation before starting. Judge engagement, evaluated progress and coach leverage independently of marketing claims.

## Decisions requested

Approve merchant/test-account discovery, one mapped program/cohort, authorized content and voice policies, pilot baseline, responsible owners and a documented operating-cost ceiling. No commercial availability, RTO/RPO, concurrency or savings guarantee until independent proof.
