# SPEC 014 — Andragogical Progress Narrative

## Outcome

Progress is presented to an adult learner as capability, application and next relevant use—not as points, streaks, mascots or school-like rewards.

## Adult progress questions

The progress surface answers:

1. What can I do now?
2. Where did I apply it?
3. What will I demonstrate next?
4. Why is that next situation relevant?

## Interaction

The progress surface uses a premium depth-card composition inspired by card-spread and 3D interaction patterns, but retains familiar buttons, tabs, focus states and reduced-motion behavior.

## Information model

### Demonstrated capability

An observable capability in plain language.

### Transfer evidence

A concrete novel/contextual situation where the capability appeared.

### Next demonstration

The next realistic use, with reduced support or changed context.

## Prohibitions

- points as the primary progress story;
- streak manipulation;
- level-up confetti;
- child-oriented badges;
- raw Twin scores on the coachee surface;
- opaque progress claims.

## Acceptance criteria

1. `/learn` contains a `Tu progreso` capability narrative.
2. It presents demonstrated capability, transfer and next demonstration.
3. Every card has an accessible tab relationship.
4. It works without motion.
5. It introduces no Learning Twin leakage to the coachee.
6. Mobile uses a clear one-column hierarchy.
7. Progress remains grounded in an observed practice event.
